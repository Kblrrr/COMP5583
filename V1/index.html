Coursework

CampusRoute

Indoor wayfinding across the three connected PolyU buildings.

Purpose. New students lose 10–15 minutes finding labs that sit behind two or three connected blocks. CampusRoute gives turn-by-turn indoor directions instead of a static floor plan.

My contribution. I designed the floor-graph data model (412 nodes, 590 edges), wrote the A* router with stair and lift penalties, and built the map screen in React Native. I coordinated a four-person team and wrote the test plan.

React Native, TypeScript, SQLite, MapLibre.

Proposed

Lecture Lens

Turning recorded lectures into timestamped, searchable notes.

Purpose. A three-hour recording is hard to revise from. Lecture Lens produces structured notes where every bullet links back to the second it came from.

My contribution. This is a proposed project, so my contribution is the design work that exists today: the proposal and evaluation plan, the note schema, and a working prototype that runs Whisper and a summariser over a 20-minute sample.

Python, Whisper, ffmpeg, LLM summarisation.

Coursework

Air, by District

An AQHI dashboard for Hong Kong’s 18 districts.

Purpose. The official AQHI page reports one number per general station. This dashboard shows the gap between districts and how it moves across a week.

My contribution. I built the ETL job over the EPD open-data feed, designed a colour-blind-safe sequential palette, and wrote the D3 charts and the small comparison view.

Python, D3, Svelte.
